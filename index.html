Why MuTC

Built for how OTC actually happens

Escrow that can't be rugged

Both sides deposit into chain-native escrow — P2WSH on Bitcoin, smart contracts on EVM and TRON, PDA programs on Solana. The swap fires automatically when both deposits confirm. Front-running the handoff is structurally impossible.

Walk away, whole

Counterparty never deposits? After the deadline, claim your funds back in full — no conditions, no support ticket. They take the reputation hit, not you.

Reputation, not paperwork

No KYC. Every trader carries a public track record — completed trades, volume, and a no-deposit rate that makes flakes visible before you commit.

Board or back-channel

Post to the open order board for standard size, or negotiate blocks privately in chat. Both routes settle through the same escrow — the safety is identical.

How it works

Three steps. No trust required.

01

Agree terms

Take an order off the board or negotiate directly. Rate, size, and deposit deadline are fixed at agreement.

02

Both sides deposit

Funds lock into escrow on each asset's own chain. Confirmations are tracked live — nothing moves until both sides are in.

03

Swap executes

The moment both deposits confirm, the swap fires automatically. Fee is deducted at execution — 0.40%, flat.

And if step 2 never completes? You reclaim your deposit in full after the deadline. The no-show takes a strike on their public record.
